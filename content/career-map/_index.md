A working path from infrastructure operations to DevOps and applied AI engineering.

## Starting point

My foundation comes from mechanical engineering, systems administration, and network deployments. Those roles built a practical understanding of how infrastructure behaves under real operational pressure.

## Current direction

The next stage is a deliberate specialization in DevOps and AI engineering, combining cloud fundamentals with repeatable delivery, observability, and responsible AI practices.

## Near-term milestones

- Complete AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) preparation.
- Complete AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) preparation.
- Build and document infrastructure experiments with AWS and Terraform.
- Publish architecture notes, deployment walkthroughs, and postmortems.

## Longer-term practice

Develop production-minded systems that are secure, observable, cost-aware, and understandable by the people who operate them.
