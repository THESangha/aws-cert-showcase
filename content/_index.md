## Hi!

I'm Sangharsh — mechanical engineer by training, systems thinker by nature.
My path here wasn't a straight line: I started in mechanical engineering, moved into systems
administration, then led network deployments — years spent in the unglamorous, support-heavy work
of keeping infrastructure running. That work taught me how systems actually fail in production, which is
a lesson no course teaches.
Now I'm converting that into a deliberate specialization: DevOps and AI engineering. This site
documents that shift — every certification, every project, every mistake — in public, as it happens.
Currently working toward: AWS Certified AI Practitioner + AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner
